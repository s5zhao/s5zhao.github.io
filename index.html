01 — Research研究方向

Positioning beyond GNSS面向无卫星信号环境的定位

Construct a stable and reliable position reference in GNSS-denied environments by leveraging environmental field information and underwater measurements.

利用环境场信息与水下测量，在无卫星信号环境中构建稳定、可靠的位置基准。

01

Inertial navigation惯性导航

Error modeling, filtering, and motion-constraint strategies that sustain navigation accuracy over long missions.

通过误差建模、滤波与运动约束，提升长航时任务中的导航精度。

INS · Filtering惯导 · 滤波
02

Geophysical field matching地球物理场匹配导航

Terrain and gravity matching methods, adaptability analysis, and matching-area selection for robust underwater navigation.

面向水下导航的地形与重力匹配方法、适配性分析及适配区选择。

Terrain · Gravity地形 · 重力
03

Multi-source fusion多源融合导航

Integrated estimation using complementary sensors and environmental observations for dependable localization.

融合互补传感器与环境观测信息，实现更加可靠的水下定位。

Fusion · Estimation融合 · 估计
